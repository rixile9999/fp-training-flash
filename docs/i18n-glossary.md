# Translation glossary and style (ko → en / zh)

Use these terms consistently in UI, content, notes and coach output. Simplified Chinese only.

## Tone

| locale | tone |
|---|---|
| ko | 해요체, concise. Source language. |
| en | Friendly, concise, second person ("you"). Plain words; no marketing tone. Sentence case for titles. |
| zh | 简体中文，友好、简洁，称呼用“你”。技术术语用下表译法，首次出现可在括号中附英文。标点使用中文全角标点，代码与英文标识符前后不加多余空格规则：中文与英文/数字之间加一个半角空格。 |
